Place your own raw tensor file in this folder.

START_HERE.txt uses my_tensor.npy as its example filename. Use your actual
filename and correct extension when running the command. Renaming a CSV,
spreadsheet, or sequencing file to .npy does not convert it into a tensor.

Supported formats and required tensor structure are explained in START_HERE.txt
and README.md. No measurement data is included in this folder.

You may also keep your tensor elsewhere and give its full path to the command.
